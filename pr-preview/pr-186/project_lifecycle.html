Project Lifecycle

Our lifecycle policy explained.

Each NeoNephos project is classified into a maturity stage as defined in our Project Lifecycle Document. Stages define benefits a project receives and obligations it must fulfill as part of the stage requirements. When applying to have your project integrated into NeoNephos, read up carefully below on the benefits and obligations each stage levies upon the projects.

Our Lifecycle Stages

Stage 0

Sandbox

Initial entry stage for new projects looking to experiment.

Stage 1

Incubation

Projects with growing adoption and active development.

Stage 2

Growth

Established projects with broad adoption and proven stability.

Stage 3

Graduated

Mature projects operating at scale with strong ecosystem integration.

Stage 4

Emeritus

Retired or inactive projects preserved for reference.
