Project Hosting Benefits

Why host a project with us?

To open source a project is just one step of the way. Establishing it in the ecosystem and building a community around it is the hard part. This is what the NeoNephos Foundation is for.

NNF Project Hosting Benefits

The NeoNephos Foundation unlocks your project's full potential by providing various services that go beyond "code":

  • Vendor-neutral intellectual property (IP) and trademark management.
  • Marketing, event hosting, and technical infrastructure support.
  • Benefit from the NeoNephos ecosystem and its partners.
  • Access to the vast Linux Foundation network and resources like LFX Insights or Project Control Center.
  • Access to structured community governance policies and contributor charters.

Neutral governance generates trust.

Q: What makes your organization most likely to adopt an open source project?

  • Foundation hosted - neutral body (52%)
  • Company backed - vendor-led projects (31%)
  • Community maintained - no institutional backing (17%)

Based on NNF co-sponsored World of Open Source Survey. Excluded answers with no preference.
