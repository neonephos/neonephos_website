As alluded to in [Advancing Europe's Open Source Agenda through Open Source Software Foundations](https://www.techzine.eu/experts/devops/139288/advancing-europes-public-agenda-through-open-source-software-foundations/), supranational sovereignty cannot be forced upon but must be build through community acceptance.
Such community acceptance requires trust, which is earned in spades but can easily be lost in buckets. [Especially in the age of AI](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-and-industry-leaders-launch-akrites-to-defend-critical-open-source-software-against-ai-enabled-cyber-threats), enterprise hardened open source software [demonstrably benefits](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/resources/publications/linux-foundation-annual-report-2025) from a structured development umbrella: Even "simple" projects nowadays often require infrastructure, token credits, a formalized vulnerability management process etc. to gain any meaningful traction in the community. These are all things which may be prohibitive for a small group of hobbyist maintainers. 


Transcends borders.
