# mogenius joins NeoNephos as a General Member

**We are pleased to welcome mogenius as a General Member of the NeoNephos Foundation.**

mogenius, a Cologne-based company develops a Kubernetes platform that enables engineering teams to operate cluster fleets across public cloud, on-premises, and air-gapped environments. The company also maintains the Renovate Operator, an open-source project that helps automate dependency updates on Kubernetes.
As NeoNephos continues to foster an open, interoperable, and sovereign cloud-to-edge ecosystem, mogenius brings valuable operational expertise and practical experience from running cloud-native platforms in diverse environments.

Their perspective on day-to-day platform operations, automation, and Kubernetes-based infrastructure will be a valuable addition to the NeoNephos community.

We look forward to collaborating with mogenius and the broader ecosystem to advance open, interoperable, and cloud-native infrastructure.

Check them out [here](https://mogenius.com).
