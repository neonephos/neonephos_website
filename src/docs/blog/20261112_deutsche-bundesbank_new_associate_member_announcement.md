# Deutsche Bundesbank joins NeoNephos as an Associate Member

We are pleased to welcome the **[Deutsche Bundesbank](https://www.bundesbank.de/de)** as an Associate Member of the **NeoNephos Foundation**.

The Deutsche Bundesbank is the central bank of the Federal Republic of Germany and forms part of the Eurosystem. Its responsibilities span monetary policy and other areas supporting a stable and reliable financial system.

As NeoNephos Foundation continues to bring together public institutions, technology providers, open-source projects, and other organizations, the Bundesbank adds an important perspective from the financial and public-sector landscape.

We look forward to the exchange and collaboration with the Deutsche Bundesbank and the broader NeoNephos community.
