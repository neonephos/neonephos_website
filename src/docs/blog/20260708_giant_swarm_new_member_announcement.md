# Giant Swarm joins NeoNephos as a General Member

We are pleased to welcome Giant Swarm as a General Member of the NeoNephos Foundation.

Since 2014, Giant Swarm has been helping organizations deploy, manage, and scale cloud-native applications through enterprise-grade Kubernetes infrastructure across cloud and on-premises environments. With deep expertise in Kubernetes, platform engineering, and cloud-native operations, Giant Swarm supports organizations in building and running reliable, scalable software platforms.

As NeoNephos continues to advance an open, interoperable, and sovereign cloud-to-edge ecosystem, Giant Swarm's practical experience in delivering production-ready cloud-native platforms will be a valuable contribution to our community.

**We look forward to collaborating with Giant Swarm and the broader community to advance open innovation and strengthen the future of cloud-native infrastructure.**
