# NeoNephos at the Open Source Summit Europe

At the start of the month, on the 7th of October, we were right in the middle of the [Open Source Summit in Prague](https://neonephos.org/events/2026_10_07_Open_Source_Summit_Europe). Connecting with our peers always gets our spirits up and this time was no different! The enthusiasm was especially apparent at our NeoNephos booth, where we met many new and old familiar faces. It is important to note that this was a cross-organizational effort, with people from multiple of our members represented at the booth.
A special highlight was CTO of Business AI Platform (BAIP) Fabric, SAP - Tarek Becker’s keynote, “Sovereignty Requires Real Choice.” In the session, Tarek discussed digital sovereignty, the importance of real technological choice, and the role open source can play in reducing dependencies. The keynote also brought NeoNephos to the main stage: Tarek introduced the NeoNephos Foundation, its mission, and the open-source projects contributing to an interoperable and sovereign cloud ecosystem.

Thank you, Prague!
