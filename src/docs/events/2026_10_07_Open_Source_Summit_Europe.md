# NeoNephos at the Open Source Summit in Prague 2026
From October 7-9, the open-source community will gather in Prague for Open Source Summit Europe 2026.
As a Silver Sponsor, the NeoNephos Foundation will be present with booth activities, project demos, and community members from across our ecosystem.
Visit us on Floor 3, Booth 51, where you'll be able to meet contributors and learn more about projects such as **CobaltCore**, **IronCore**, **Open Control Plane**, **Open Component Model (OCM)**, and **Chantico**.

The event will also feature the NeoNephos-related talk:

**"Alert Fatigue is a Security Vulnerability: Why Most CVEs Don't Matter and How to Find the Ones That Do"**

Presented by contributors from the **CobaltCore project**, the session explores practical approaches to reducing vulnerability noise and helping teams focus on the findings that truly matter.

We look forward to meeting the wider open-source community, sharing ideas, showcasing our projects, and discussing the future of open, interoperable infrastructure.
