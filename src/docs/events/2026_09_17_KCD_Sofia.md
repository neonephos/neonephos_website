## Kubernetes Community Days Sofia 2026
Kubernetes Community Days (KCD) Sofia returns on **September 29, 2026**, bringing together the cloud-native community for a day of technical talks, networking, and knowledge sharing around Kubernetes, platform engineering, observability, security, and open source.
The event is organized by the local CNCF community and has become one of the leading cloud-native community events in the region.
 

### NeoNephos at KCD Sofia 
We are pleased that **Orlin Vasilev** will represent the **NeoNephos Foundation** at this year's event. 
KCD Sofia provides an opportunity to exchange ideas with cloud-native practitioners, contributors, and organizations from across the ecosystem while discussing topics such as interoperability, open-source collaboration, and digital sovereignty.
