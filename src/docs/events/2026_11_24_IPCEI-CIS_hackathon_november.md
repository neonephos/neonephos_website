## IPCEI-CIS Hackathon Potsdam

**On 24-26 November** we again bring together contributors and representatives of our projects for a multi-day hackathon at the SAP Innovation Center in Potsdam. The previous edition of the IPCEI-CIS Hackathon series which occurred this July counted around 100 participants from various organizations and this time it [appears to be no different](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/pull/98#issuecomment-6980161897). 

The themes are diverse, with some being a continuation of the previous hackathon. Foe example, there is a focus on bare metal operations via [IronCore](https://ironcore.dev/), [Open Component Model](https://ocm.software/) as delivery framework, [Platform Mesh](https://platform-mesh.io/) for service interoperability. Bonus: Even our [newly established](https://neonephos.org/blog/20261118_thalamus_new_project) standalone project [Thalamus](https://cobaltcore-dev.github.io/thalamus/v0.2.0/) is present.

### Why is it called IPCEI-CIS Hackathon?

The hackathon is hosted under the IPCEI-CIS umbrella because many NNF projects [are supported by this initiative](https://apeirora.eu/). This hackathon is part of a wider effort to promote and establish NeoNephos projects in the European ecoystem.
