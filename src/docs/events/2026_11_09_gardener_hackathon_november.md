## Hack The Garden 11/2026

**Hack The Garden 11/2026** brings together contributors, maintainers, and users from across the Gardener ecosystem for several days of focused collaboration, hands-on development, and community building.

The event is intentionally kept small, with a maximum of 28 participants, creating an environment where attendees can work closely together on real challenges, share knowledge, and actively contribute to the future of the project.

### NeoNephos and Hack The Garden

We are pleased that the **NeoNephos Foundation** is supporting this edition of Hack The Garden by sponsoring half of the event costs.

Supporting community-driven events such as Hack The Garden helps strengthen the open-source ecosystem around Gardener and related infrastructure projects. It also creates opportunities for maintainers, contributors, and adopters to collaborate directly, exchange ideas, and shape future development priorities together.

As one of the key projects within the NeoNephos ecosystem, Gardener plays an important role in enabling open, interoperable, and cloud-native infrastructure. Investing in its community helps foster long-term sustainability, trust, and collaboration across the ecosystem.

Whether you are an experienced contributor or looking to become more engaged with the Gardener community, Hack The Garden offers a unique opportunity to connect with peers and work together on the future of open infrastructure.
