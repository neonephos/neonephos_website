# Project Community Managers Sync

A recurring, peer-led space for the people doing the quiet, essential work behind every NeoNephos project — welcoming contributors, keeping communities engaged, and figuring out what actually works.

Every project is solving the same problems in parallel. This sync exists so we stop solving them alone.

## What this is

- **Peer-led and bi-directional.** No presentations. No reporting up. No imposed agenda.
- **A working session, not a status meeting.** Bring your real challenges, your experiments, your wins — we work through them together.
- **Community management as shared infrastructure.** The techniques that make one project's onboarding smooth are almost certainly useful for another. When we share, we compound.

## What to bring

Come prepared to share, not just to listen:

- A challenge you're stuck on
- An experiment that worked — or didn't
- A pattern you've noticed
- A question you've been sitting on because there was nobody obvious to ask

The more openly we exchange, the more useful this becomes for all of us.

## Who this is for

Community managers, DevRels, maintainers wearing a community hat — anyone across NeoNephos projects doing the work of growing and sustaining a contributor community.

## Interested in joining us?

If you care about technical community work and want to be part of this, the starting point is simple: **subscribe to our mailing list at [lists.neonephos.org/g/nnf-project-community-managers](https://lists.neonephos.org/g/nnf-project-community-managers) and join the meetings.** That's it — no formal application, no gatekeeping. Show up, share what you're working on, and help us build something useful together.
