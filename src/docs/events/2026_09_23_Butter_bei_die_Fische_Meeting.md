## Butter bei die Fische - Lets get down to business!
Several NNF members met in Hamburg (thus the "Butter bei die Fische")  and organically identified a potential opportunity to work together, and we now want to bring that idea to the wider community.
The proposal is to put digital sovereignty into practice by building an end-to-end environment where different organizations each contribute a piece.
We want to test whether independently provided open source components can work together across providers and environments.

The focus is on interoperability, scale, portability, federation, and creating agency.

This meeting is an open discussion to hear the proposals and ideas, challenge it, and decide together whether and how to take it forward.
