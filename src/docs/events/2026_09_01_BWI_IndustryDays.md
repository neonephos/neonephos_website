## BWI Industry Days 2026

The **BWI Industry Days 2026** will bring together experts from industry, government, and technology to discuss the ongoing digital transformation of the German Armed Forces and the future of sovereign digital infrastructure.

As part of the event, **Vasu Chandrasekhara** will represent the **NeoNephos Foundation** in the **Digital Sovereignty** breakout session. The session will feature an impulse talk on NeoNephos, followed by a panel discussion on the future of digital sovereignty and the role of open ecosystems, interoperability, and collaboration in building resilient digital infrastructure.

We look forward to contributing to this important conversation and engaging with stakeholders from across the public and private sectors.

### Event Involvement from NeoNephos

**Vasu Chandrasekhara** - NeoNephos Foundation:

 **Breakout Session: Digital Sovereignty**
- 12:00 - 12:10 - Impulse Talk: *NeoNephos Foundation*
- 12:10 - 12:30 - Panel Discussion: *What's Next for Digital Sovereignty?*
