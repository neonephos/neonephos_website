## NeoNephos at Fieldlab Soevereine Basis 2026

From **November 23–25, 2026, the Fieldlab Soevereine Basis** brings Dutch government teams together in The Hague for three days of hands-on work around sovereign digital infrastructure.

Participants will work on practical scenarios ranging from deploying applications on a sovereign cloud proof of concept to building platform integrations, monitoring, SSO, compliance tooling, and testing a sovereign digital workplace.

## NeoNephos at the Fieldlab

**Markus Fensterer**, NeoNephos Governing Board member from x-cellent technologies, will represent the Foundation and share the perspective of metal-stack.

His talk will explore why metal-stack chose the NeoNephos Foundation as the neutral home for its open-source project and how technologies such as metal-stack and Gardener contribute to building sovereign cloud infrastructure.

For NeoNephos, the Fieldlab provides a practical setting to exchange experiences around open source, interoperability, and the technologies needed to turn digital sovereignty into working infrastructure.
