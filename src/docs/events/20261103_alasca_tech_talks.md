# NeoNephos at ALASCA Summit 2026

On **November 3–4, 2026**, the open-source community will come together in Dresden for the **ALASCA Summit 2026**.

This year's motto, **“With C – as in Community. Breaking down technical silos united for a digitally sovereign Europe.”**, puts collaboration at the center of the event. The program combines technical deep dives, hands-on workshops, panel discussions, networking, and an OpenInfra community track.

## NeoNephos at ALASCA Summit

**Christian Neu** will represent the **NeoNephos Foundation** in the panel discussion:

### With C – as in Community. Breaking down technical silos united for a digitally sovereign Europe.

Together with representatives from politics and industry, the panel will discuss how collaboration across organizations, technologies, and communities can help break down silos and contribute to a digitally sovereign Europe.

🕛 **12:00–13:00**

🗣️ **Language: English**
